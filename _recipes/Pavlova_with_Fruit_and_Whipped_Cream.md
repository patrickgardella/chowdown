ingredients:
- "**Meringue:**"
- 1 1/2 cups (10 1/2 oz) sugar
- 3/4 cup (6 oz) egg whites (5 to 7 large eggs)
- 1/2 tsp distilled white vinegar
- 1/2 tsp cornstarch
- 1 tsp vanilla extract
- "**Whipped cream:**"
- 2 cups heavy cream, chilled
- 2 Tbsp sugar
- "**Fruit topping (choose one — see note):**"
- Fresh fruit, prepared

directions:
- Adjust oven rack to middle position and heat oven to 250°F. Using a pencil, draw a 10-inch circle in the center of an 18 by 13-inch piece of parchment paper.
- Combine sugar and egg whites in the bowl of a stand mixer. Place bowl over a saucepan filled with 1 inch of simmering water (water must not touch the bowl). Whisking gently but constantly, heat until sugar is dissolved and mixture registers 160 to 165°F, 5 to 8 minutes.
- Fit stand mixer with the whisk attachment and whip mixture on high speed until it forms stiff peaks, is smooth, and is bright white with a sheen, about 4 minutes. Scrape down bowl with a spatula. Add vinegar, cornstarch, and vanilla and whip on high speed until combined, about 10 seconds.
- Spoon about 1/4 teaspoon meringue onto each corner of a rimmed baking sheet. Press the parchment, marked side down, onto the sheet to secure. Pile meringue in center of circle. Using the circle as a guide, spread and smooth meringue from center outward, building a 10-inch disk that is slightly higher around the edges with a 1/4-inch depression in the center. The finished disk should measure about 1 inch high.
- Bake until exterior is dry and crisp and meringue releases cleanly from parchment when gently lifted at the edge with a thin spatula, 1 to 1 1/2 hours. The meringue should be quite pale. Turn off the oven, prop door open with a wooden spoon, and let meringue cool in oven for 1 1/2 hours. Remove from oven and let cool completely, about 15 more minutes. (Cooled meringue can be wrapped tightly in plastic and stored at room temperature up to 1 week.)
- "**For the whipped cream:** Whip cream and sugar in chilled bowl of stand mixer fitted with whisk attachment, starting on low speed until small bubbles form, about 30 seconds, then medium until whisk leaves a trail, about 30 seconds more, then high until cream is smooth, thick, and nearly doubled in volume. Finish by hand if needed."
- Carefully peel meringue from parchment and place on a large serving platter. Spoon whipped cream into center. Top with fruit topping. Let stand for at least 5 minutes or up to 1 hour, then slice and serve.

---

Fruit toppings: (1) Mango, kiwi, and blueberry — toss 3 cups mango, 1 cup kiwi, and 1 cup blueberries with 1 Tbsp sugar; set aside 30 minutes. (2) Orange, cranberry, and mint — make a simple syrup, poach cranberries, drain and sugar-coat half; toss oranges with remaining cranberries and chopped mint, top pavlova, garnish with sugared cranberries. Do not worry when meringue cracks — it is part of the dessert's charm.
