ingredients:
- "**Dough:**"
- 3 cups (16 1/2 oz) bread flour
- 2 Tbsp sugar
- 1/2 tsp instant or rapid-rise yeast
- 1 1/3 cups ice water
- 1 Tbsp vegetable oil
- 1 1/2 tsp table salt
- "**Sauce:**"
- 1 (28-oz) can whole peeled tomatoes, drained
- 1 Tbsp extra-virgin olive oil
- 1 tsp red wine vinegar
- 2 garlic cloves, minced
- 1 tsp table salt
- 1 tsp dried oregano
- 1/4 tsp pepper
- "**Pizza:**"
- 1 oz Parmesan cheese, grated fine (1/2 cup), divided
- 8 oz whole-milk mozzarella, shredded (2 cups), divided

directions:
- "**Dough:** Pulse flour, sugar, and yeast in food processor until combined, about 5 pulses. With processor running, slowly add ice water; process until just combined and no dry flour remains, about 10 seconds. Let sit for 10 minutes. Add oil and salt and process until dough forms a satiny, sticky ball and clears sides of bowl, 30 to 60 seconds. Transfer to lightly oiled counter and knead briefly until smooth, about 1 minute. Shape into tight ball and place in lightly oiled large bowl; cover tightly with plastic wrap and refrigerate for at least 24 hours or up to 3 days."
- "**Sauce:** Process tomatoes, oil, vinegar, garlic, salt, oregano, and pepper in clean, dry workbowl until smooth, about 30 seconds. Transfer to bowl and refrigerate until ready to use. (Extra sauce can be refrigerated up to 1 week or frozen up to 1 month.)"
- One hour before baking, adjust oven rack to upper-middle position (4 to 5 inches from broiler), set baking stone on rack, and heat oven to 500°F. Remove dough from refrigerator, divide in half, and shape each half into a tight ball. Place balls on lightly greased baking sheet, spacing at least 3 inches apart; cover loosely with greased plastic and let sit for 1 hour.
- "**Shape and bake:** Coat 1 ball of dough generously with flour and place on well-floured counter (keep other ball covered). Using your fingertips, gently flatten dough into 8-inch disk, leaving 1 inch of edge slightly thicker than center. Using your hands, gently stretch disk into 12-inch round. Transfer to well-floured pizza peel and stretch into 13-inch round."
- Spread 1/2 cup sauce in thin layer over dough, leaving 1/4-inch border. Sprinkle 1/4 cup Parmesan evenly over sauce, followed by 1 cup mozzarella. Slide pizza carefully onto baking stone and bake until crust is well browned and cheese is bubbly and beginning to brown, 10 to 12 minutes, rotating pizza halfway through. Transfer to wire rack and let cool 5 minutes before serving. Repeat to shape, top, and bake second pizza.

---

Use ice water in the dough to prevent overheating in the food processor. Semolina flour is ideal for dusting the peel. Shape the second pizza while the first bakes, but do not top until right before baking.
