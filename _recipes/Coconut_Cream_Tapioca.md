ingredients:
- 4 T Minute Tapioca
- 4 T sugar
- 1/4 t salt
- 2 egg yolks
- 2 cups milk
- 1/2 cup shredded coconut, divided
- 2 egg whites
- 4 T sugar (for meringue)

directions:
- Combine Minute Tapioca, 4T sugar, salt, egg yolks, and milk in the top of a double boiler. Stir enough to break the egg yolks.
- Place over rapidly boiling water, bring to scalding point (allow 5–7 minutes) and cook 5 minutes, stirring frequently.
- Stir in all but a few tablespoons of coconut. Pour into a greased baking dish.
- Beat egg whites until foamy. Add remaining 4T sugar, 2T at a time, beating after each addition until thoroughly blended. Continue beating until stiff peaks form.
- Pile meringue lightly on the tapioca mixture. Sprinkle with remaining coconut.
- Bake in a moderate oven (350°F) for 15 minutes or until golden brown.
