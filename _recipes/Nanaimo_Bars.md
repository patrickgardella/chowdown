ingredients:
- "**Base:**"
- 2 cups graham cracker crumbs
- 1 cup coconut
- 1/2 cup chopped walnuts
- 1/2 cup butter
- 1/4 cup sugar
- 5 Tbsp cocoa powder
- 1 tsp vanilla
- 1 egg
- "**Custard layer:**"
- 1/4 cup butter
- 2 cups powdered sugar
- 2 Tbsp custard powder
- 3 Tbsp milk
- "**Chocolate top:**"
- 4 oz semisweet chocolate (8 squares)
- 2 1/2 Tbsp butter

directions:
- Mix graham crackers, coconut, and nuts in a bowl. Combine butter, sugar, cocoa, vanilla, and egg in a pot and heat and stir until cooked. Mix chocolate mixture with wafer mixture and mix well. Press into a 9x13 pan lined with parchment paper and refrigerate.
- Cream sugar and butter. Combine milk and custard powder. Beat custard mixture into sugar and butter until smooth. Spread on top of base and refrigerate.
- Cut the chocolate squares into shards. Melt butter in a small pot and add the chocolate. When completely melted, spread on top of custard layer. Cool in refrigerator.
- When the chocolate is no longer glossy, cut through it (otherwise it cracks when cut when fully hardened). Cut into squares — usually makes about 6 x 8 squares.
