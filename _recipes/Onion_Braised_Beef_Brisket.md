ingredients:
- 1 (4- to 5-lb) beef brisket, flat cut, fat trimmed to 1/4 inch
- 2 1/4 tsp table salt, divided
- 1 tsp pepper
- 1 tsp vegetable oil, plus extra as needed
- 2 1/2 lbs onions, halved and sliced 1/2 inch thick
- 1 Tbsp packed brown sugar
- 3 garlic cloves, minced
- 1 Tbsp tomato paste
- 1 Tbsp paprika
- 1/8 tsp cayenne pepper
- 2 Tbsp all-purpose flour
- 1 cup chicken broth
- 1 cup dry red wine
- 3 bay leaves
- 3 sprigs fresh thyme
- 2 tsp cider vinegar

directions:
- Adjust oven rack to lower-middle position and heat oven to 300°F. Line 13 by 9-inch baking dish with two 24-inch-long sheets of 18-inch-wide heavy-duty aluminum foil, positioning sheets perpendicular to each other with excess extending beyond edges of dish. Pat brisket dry with paper towels. Using a fork, poke holes in meat through fat layer about 1 inch apart. Season both sides with 2 teaspoons salt and pepper.
- Heat oil in 12-inch skillet over medium-high heat until just smoking. Place brisket fat side up in skillet; weight with heavy Dutch oven or cast-iron skillet and cook until well browned, about 7 minutes. Remove Dutch oven; flip brisket and cook on second side, without weight, until well browned, about 7 minutes. Transfer to platter.
- Pour off all but 1 tablespoon fat from skillet. Add onions, sugar, and remaining 1/4 teaspoon salt; cook over medium-high heat, stirring occasionally, until onions are softened, 10 to 12 minutes. Add garlic and cook until fragrant, about 1 minute. Stir in tomato paste and cook until paste darkens, about 2 minutes. Add paprika and cayenne and cook until fragrant, about 1 minute. Add flour and cook, stirring constantly, until well combined, about 2 minutes. Stir in broth, wine, bay leaves, and thyme, scraping up browned bits; bring to simmer and cook for 5 minutes to thicken.
- Pour sauce and onions into prepared dish. Nestle brisket fat side up into sauce and onions. Fold foil extensions over brisket and seal (do not tightly crimp). Bake until fork slips easily in and out of meat, 3 1/2 to 4 hours. Carefully open foil and let brisket cool for 20 to 30 minutes.
- Transfer brisket to large bowl; set fine-mesh strainer over bowl and strain sauce over brisket. Discard bay leaves and thyme; transfer onions to small bowl. Cover both bowls with plastic wrap, cut vents, and refrigerate for at least 8 hours or up to 2 days.
- About 45 minutes before serving, adjust oven rack to lower-middle position and heat oven to 350°F. Transfer brisket to carving board. Skim fat from surface of sauce, then transfer sauce to medium saucepan and reheat over medium heat until warm, skimming any more fat (you should have about 2 cups sauce). Slice brisket against grain 1/4 inch thick and place in 13 by 9-inch baking dish. Stir in vinegar and reserved onions and season with salt and pepper. Pour sauce over brisket, cover with foil, and bake until heated through, 25 to 30 minutes. Serve immediately.

---

Increase the cayenne to 1/4 teaspoon for a spicier sauce. You will need 18-inch-wide heavy-duty foil.
