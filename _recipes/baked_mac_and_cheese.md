ingredients:

- 8 oz dry elbow macaroni, shells, or cavatappi
- 2 T butter
- 2 T all purpose flour
- 1 1/3 c milk (not skim)
- 1/3 t salt
- 1/3 t pepper
- 1 1/3 or 5.3 oz shredded cheese

directions:
- preheat oven to 350
- boil water with salt and cook pasta as directed
- while the pasta cooks, melt butter in a skillet large enough to contain the pasta
- add the floor and stir over medium heat until the mixture is lightly browned (1-2 minutes) 
- add the milk and whisk to remove lumps, then add salt and pepper
- cook over medium -high heat until the sauce thickens and starts to bubble (6 minutes)
- stir in the cheese and whisk until smooth and melted. turn off heat
- when the pasta is almost done but still firm, drain and add to sauce
- stir pasta into the sauce and bake in a greased 8x8 pan for 20-25 minutes until browned and bubbly
