ingredients:
- "**Lemon Sugar-Almond Topping:**"
- 1/4 cup sugar
- 1/2 tsp finely grated lemon zest
- 1/2 cup sliced almonds
- "**Cake:**"
- 2 1/4 cups all-purpose flour
- 1 1/8 tsp baking powder
- 1/8 tsp baking soda
- 1 tsp table salt
- 10 Tbsp unsalted butter, softened
- 1 1/8 cups plus 5 Tbsp sugar, divided
- 1 Tbsp finely grated lemon zest plus 4 tsp lemon juice
- 4 large eggs
- 5 tsp vanilla extract, divided
- 1/4 cup sour cream
- 8 oz cream cheese, softened

directions:
- Adjust oven rack to middle position and heat oven to 350°F. Stir together 1/4 cup sugar and lemon zest in small bowl until combined. Stir in almonds; set aside.
- Spray a 10-inch tube pan with vegetable oil spray. Whisk flour, baking powder, baking soda, and salt together in bowl; set aside. In stand mixer fitted with paddle, beat butter, 1 cup plus 2 tablespoons sugar, and lemon zest at medium speed until light and fluffy, about 3 minutes. Add eggs one at a time, beating well after each addition. Add 4 teaspoons vanilla and mix to combine. Reduce speed to low and add flour mixture in 3 additions, alternating with sour cream in 2 additions, mixing until just incorporated. Remove bowl and fold batter once or twice with spatula to incorporate any remaining flour.
- Measure out 1 1/4 cups batter and set aside. Spoon remaining batter into prepared pan and smooth top. Beat cream cheese, remaining 5 tablespoons sugar, lemon juice, and remaining 1 teaspoon vanilla in now-empty bowl on medium speed until smooth and slightly lightened, about 1 minute. Add 1/4 cup of reserved batter and mix until incorporated.
- Spoon cream cheese filling evenly over batter, keeping filling about 1 inch from edges of pan; smooth top. Spread remaining 1 cup reserved batter over filling and smooth top. Gently swirl filling into batter using a figure-8 motion with a butter knife, being careful not to drag filling to the bottom or edges of the pan. Firmly tap pan on counter 2 or 3 times to dislodge any bubbles. Sprinkle lemon sugar-almond topping evenly over batter and gently press into batter to adhere.
- Bake until top is golden and just firm and a skewer inserted into the cake comes out clean, 45 to 50 minutes. Remove from oven and tap firmly on counter 2 or 3 times. Let cool in pan on wire rack for 1 hour. Gently invert cake onto rimmed baking sheet; remove tube pan, place wire rack on top, and invert cake right side up. Let cool about 1 1/2 hours before serving. Store covered tightly in the refrigerator; bring to room temperature before serving.
