ingredients:
- 1 1/2 cups beef broth, divided
- 1/2 oz dried porcini mushrooms, rinsed
- 1 Tbsp extra-virgin olive oil
- 1 onion, chopped fine
- 2 garlic cloves, minced
- 1 Tbsp tomato paste
- 3 anchovy fillets, rinsed, patted dry, and minced
- 1/2 tsp five-spice powder
- 1/2 cup dry red wine
- 1 (14.5-oz) can whole peeled tomatoes, drained with juice reserved, chopped fine
- 2 lbs boneless beef short ribs, trimmed
- 3/4 tsp table salt
- 1/2 tsp pepper

directions:
- Adjust oven rack to middle position and heat oven to 350°F. Microwave 1/2 cup broth and mushrooms in covered bowl until steaming, about 1 minute. Let sit until softened, about 5 minutes. Drain mushrooms through fine-mesh strainer lined with coffee filter, pressing to extract all liquid; reserve liquid and chop mushrooms fine.
- Heat oil in Dutch oven over medium heat until shimmering. Add onion and cook, stirring occasionally, until softened, about 5 minutes. Add garlic and cook until fragrant, about 1 minute. Add tomato paste, anchovies, and five-spice powder and cook, stirring frequently, until mixture has darkened and fond forms on pot bottom, 3 to 4 minutes. Add wine, increase heat to medium-high, and bring to simmer, scraping up any browned bits.
- Continue to cook, stirring frequently, until wine is reduced and pot is almost dry, 2 to 4 minutes. Add tomatoes and reserved mushroom soaking liquid and mushrooms and bring to simmer.
- Toss beef with salt and pepper. Add beef to pot, cover, and transfer to oven. Cook for 1 hour. Uncover and continue to cook until beef is tender, 1 to 1 1/4 hours more.
- Remove pot from oven. Transfer beef to cutting board and let cool 5 minutes. Shred into bite-size pieces with 2 forks; discard any large pieces of fat or connective tissue. Skim off excess fat from surface of sauce. Return beef to sauce and season with salt and pepper to taste.

---

Serve over 1 lb pasta or a batch of No-Fuss Creamy Parmesan Polenta. Sauce can be refrigerated for up to 3 days or frozen for up to 2 months.
