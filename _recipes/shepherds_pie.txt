tags: Beef, Entree

ingredients:

- 1 1 1/2 lb ground beef
- 1/2 t garlic powder
- 1/2 t onion powder
- 1 t salt
- 1/4 t pepper
- 1 T flour
- 1 T worcestershire sauce
- 2 T ketchup
- 2 cans corn drained
- 1 bag prepared instant mashed potatoes
- 3/4 c shredded cheese

directions:
- preheat oven to 350
- brown ground beef over medium heat
- add seasonings and sauces to ground beef
- put meat  mixture into casserole dish
- cover meat with drained corn
- mix cheese into mashed potatoes
- cover corn with mashed potatoes
- sprinkle more cheese onto mashed potatoes
- cook in preheated oven for 30 minutes
