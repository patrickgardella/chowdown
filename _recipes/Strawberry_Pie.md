ingredients:
- 1 pint strawberries, hulled and cut
- 1 cooled graham cracker crust or deep-dish pie crust (9-inch)
- 1 1/2 cups water
- 3/4 cup sugar
- 2 Tbsp cornstarch mixed with 1 cup water
- 1 pkg (3 oz) strawberry Jello
- Heavy whipping cream, for serving (optional)

directions:
- Combine 1 1/2 cups water with sugar and strawberries in a saucepan. Cook over medium heat until mixture boils, stirring regularly.
- Add the cornstarch slurry to the pan. Stir and cook until the mixture thickens.
- Add the Jello packet. Simmer a few minutes while stirring until dissolved.
- Let cool a little and pour into crust. Cool completely then refrigerate until serving. Top with heavy whipping cream if desired.
