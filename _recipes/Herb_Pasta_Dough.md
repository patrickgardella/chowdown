ingredients:
- 1 Tbsp finely chopped fresh thyme leaves
- 1 Tbsp finely chopped fresh rosemary leaves
- 1 Tbsp finely chopped fresh sage leaves
- 3 cups all-purpose flour
- 5 large egg yolks
- 1 tsp kosher salt
- 1 tsp extra-virgin olive oil
- 1 Tbsp water, if needed
- Semolina or all-purpose flour, for dusting

directions:
- "**Make herb tea:** In a very small saucepan, bring 1/2 cup water to a boil. Add the herbs and bring back to a boil. Lower heat and reduce to 1/4 cup. Remove from heat, cover, and let steep for 15 minutes. Cool completely."
- In a food processor fitted with the steel blade, place flour, egg yolks, salt, olive oil, and herb tea. Process until dough begins to hold together, then pinch to test. If too dry, add 1 tablespoon water and process until it forms a moist ball. Turn out onto a lightly floured surface and knead by hand, forming a smooth ball. Loosely wrap in plastic wrap and let rest at room temperature for 30 minutes.
- Cut dough into 4 equal pieces. Keep other pieces covered in plastic while you roll out one piece at a time, either by hand with a rolling pin or through the rollers of a pasta machine.
- If using a pasta machine, set rollers at the widest opening. Flatten the first piece of dough into a thick strip no wider than the machine. Run through the rollers, fold in thirds crosswise, and run through again. Repeat 2 or more times until dough is smooth and elastic. Set machine to each successively smaller opening, dusting lightly with flour only as necessary, until you reach the next-to-last or last opening.
- Adjust the cutting mechanism to the desired width, cut the noodles, and allow them to dry before cooking. Spread cut pasta on a flour-dusted tray, sprinkle with flour, layer with parchment, and continue layering. Dry at least 15 to 20 minutes. Repeat with remaining pieces of dough.

---

Fresh pasta will keep refrigerated for up to 3 days.
