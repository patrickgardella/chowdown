ingredients:
- 1/2 cup Minute Tapioca
- 4 t sugar
- 1/2 t salt
- 1/4 t ground nutmeg
- 2 cups canned peaches, drained
- 2.5 cups water and peach juice
- 1 T lemon juice
- 2 T melted butter

directions:
- Combine all ingredients in a greased baking dish and mix thoroughly.
- Bake in a moderate oven (375°F) for 30 minutes or until done, stirring well every 10 minutes and again when removing from oven.
- Serve warm or cold with cream.
