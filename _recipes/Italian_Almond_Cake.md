ingredients:
- 1 1/2 cups plus 1/2 cup blanched sliced almonds, toasted, divided
- 3/4 cup all-purpose flour
- 3/4 tsp table salt
- 1/4 tsp baking powder
- 4 large eggs
- 1 1/4 cups plus 2 Tbsp sugar, divided
- 1 Tbsp plus 1/2 tsp grated lemon zest, divided
- 1/2 tsp almond extract
- 5 Tbsp unsalted butter, melted
- 1/3 cup vegetable oil

directions:
- Adjust oven rack to middle position and heat oven to 300°F. Grease a 9-inch round cake pan and line with parchment paper. Pulse 1/2 cup almonds, flour, salt, and baking powder in food processor until almonds are finely ground, 5 to 10 pulses. Transfer almond mixture to a bowl.
- Process eggs, 1 1/4 cups sugar, 1 tablespoon lemon zest, and almond extract in the now-empty processor until very pale yellow, about 2 minutes. With processor running, add melted butter and oil in a steady stream until incorporated, about 1 minute. Add almond mixture and pulse to combine, 4 to 5 pulses. Transfer batter to prepared pan.
- Combine remaining 2 tablespoons sugar and remaining 1/2 teaspoon lemon zest with your fingers in a small bowl until fragrant, 5 to 10 seconds. Sprinkle top of cake evenly with remaining 1/2 cup almonds followed by the sugar-zest mixture.
- Bake until center is set and bounces back when gently pressed and a toothpick inserted in center comes out clean, 55 minutes to 1 hour 5 minutes, rotating pan after 40 minutes. Let cool in pan on wire rack for 15 minutes. Run a paring knife around sides of pan, invert cake onto a greased wire rack, discard parchment, and reinvert onto a second wire rack. Let cool about 2 hours before serving.

---

Serve with Orange Crème Fraîche: combine 1 cup crème fraîche, 2 Tbsp sugar, 1/8 tsp salt, 1 tsp orange zest, and orange segments from 2 oranges cut into 1/4-inch pieces. Refrigerate 1 hour before serving. Cooled cake can be wrapped in plastic and stored at room temperature up to 3 days.
