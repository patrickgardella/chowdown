ingredients:
- 3 lbs chuck roast
- 1 onion, sliced
- 1 red pepper, sliced
- 6 cloves garlic
- 1 cup cilantro
- 1 small can tomato paste
- 2–3 bay leaves
- 1/2–1 tsp oregano
- 1/2–1 tsp cumin
- Salt and pepper
- Beef broth, as needed
- White rice and black beans, for serving

directions:
- Season chuck roast with salt and pepper. Place in a covered pot with enough broth to come partway up the meat.
- Cook covered over low heat for 3 hours, flipping every hour, until the fat renders and the meat is tender enough to shred.
- Remove meat and shred.
- Add onion, red pepper, garlic, and cilantro to the pot. If dry, add a splash of broth.
- Stir in tomato paste, cumin, bay leaves, and oregano.
- Return shredded beef to the pot and simmer 15–20 minutes until flavors meld. Remove bay leaves.
- Serve over white rice with black beans, with the braising sauce spooned over the top.
