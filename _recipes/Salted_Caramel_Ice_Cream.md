ingredients:
- 2/3 cup heavy cream
- 2 cups whole milk
- 175g + 25g granulated sugar
- 1/4 cup water
- 1 vanilla bean, split and scraped
- Kosher salt
- 5 egg yolks

directions:
- Combine heavy cream and vanilla bean (with seeds) in a medium saucepan. Warm over low heat just below a simmer to infuse the cream with vanilla. Keep warm.
- Combine 175g sugar and 1/4 cup water in a large saucepan. Heat over medium, gently stirring until clear. Use a wet pastry brush to wipe down any crystals on the sides of the pan.
- Stop stirring when the mixture boils. Swirl occasionally until the caramel turns deep amber and the bubbles slow almost to a stop — it will darken quickly at the end, so watch it closely.
- Pour the warm vanilla cream into the caramel (it will bubble rapidly). Stir gently to combine. Remove from heat and cool slightly.
- Stir in the milk over low heat until combined.
- In a bowl, whisk egg yolks with remaining 25g sugar until lightened. Gradually ladle the warm milk mixture into the yolks in 1/2-cup increments to temper.
- Return everything to the pot over medium heat. Cook, stirring constantly, until the mixture coats the back of a spoon and holds a line (170–180°F). Do not boil.
- Season with kosher salt to taste (start with 1/2 tsp). Strain through a fine-mesh strainer and cool over an ice bath.
- Churn in an ice cream maker per manufacturer's instructions. Freeze until firm.
