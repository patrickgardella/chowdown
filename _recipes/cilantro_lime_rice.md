ingredients:
- 3 c water
- 2 c rice
- 3 T butter
- 1 1/2 t salt
- 1/3 c minced cilantro or 2 1/2 T dried cilantro
- 2 T lime juice

directions:
- add rice, water, butter, and salt to rice cooker and cook as directed
- when rice is done, stir in lime juice and cilantro
