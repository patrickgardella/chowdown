ingredients:
- 2 Tbsp olive oil or vegetable oil
- 2 (6-oz) skin-on salmon fillets, patted dry

directions:
- Drizzle oil in a large nonstick skillet. Add salmon skin side down, gently moving it in the oil for even coverage. Do not preheat the pan.
- Place skillet over medium-high heat. Cook undisturbed until skin is golden brown and crispy and flesh is mostly opaque around the edges, 7–8 minutes.
- Flip carefully and remove skillet from heat. Let carryover heat bring the salmon to 125°F internal temperature, 2–3 more minutes.
- Transfer to a plate and serve immediately.

---

Starting in a cold pan renders the skin slowly and evenly, producing deeply crispy skin while keeping the flesh moist. Do not move the fish while the skin is crisping.
