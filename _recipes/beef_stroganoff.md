tags: Beef, Entree

ingredients:

- 1 1 1/2 lb ground beef
- 1 onion
- garlic
- 1 can of chicken mushroom soup
- 1/2 bay leaf
- 5 squirts of worcestershire sauce
- 1/2 t crushed red pepper
- 1 c sour cream
- salt and pepper, to taste
- wide noodles

directions:
- cook wide noodles
- sweat onions, low heat until translucent
- brown ground beef and garlic, drain grease
- add can of chicken mushroom soup, bay leaf, and worcestershire sauce, cook for 10 minutes
- add crushed red pepper and sour cream, add salt and pepper to taste, cook for 5 minutes
