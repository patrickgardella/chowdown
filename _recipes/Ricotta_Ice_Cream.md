ingredients:
- 1⅔ cups fresh, whole milk ricotta
- 3 oz cream cheese
- 1 cup whole milk
- 1 cup sugar
- 2 Tbsp dark rum
- 1 tsp lemon zest, freshly grated
- 1/2 tsp vanilla
- 1/8 tsp salt
- 1 cup heavy cream
- 2–3 Tbsp chopped candied citrus peel (orange, lemon, and citron)
- 2–3 Tbsp chopped pistachios
- 2–3 Tbsp chopped bittersweet chocolate

directions:
- Blend ricotta, cream cheese, milk, sugar, rum, lemon zest, vanilla, and salt in a blender until smooth.
- Add cream and blend until just combined.
- Freeze in an ice cream maker, adding candied citrus peel, pistachios, and chocolate near the end of churning.
- Transfer to an airtight container and freeze until firm. Let sit a few minutes before scooping.
