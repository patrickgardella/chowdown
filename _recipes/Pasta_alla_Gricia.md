ingredients:
- 8 oz pancetta, sliced 1/4 inch thick
- 1 Tbsp extra-virgin olive oil
- 1 lb rigatoni
- 1 tsp coarsely ground pepper, plus extra for serving
- 2 oz Pecorino Romano cheese, grated fine (1 cup), plus extra for serving

directions:
- Slice each round of pancetta into rectangular pieces about 1/2 inch by 1 inch. Heat pancetta and oil in Dutch oven over medium-low heat, stirring frequently, until fat is rendered and pancetta is deep golden brown but still has a slight pinkish hue, 8 to 10 minutes, adjusting heat as necessary. Using slotted spoon, transfer pancetta to bowl. Pour fat from pot into liquid measuring cup (you should have 1/4 to 1/3 cup fat; discard any extra). Return fat to Dutch oven.
- While pancetta cooks, set colander in large bowl. Bring 2 quarts water to boil in large pot. Add pasta and cook, stirring often, until al dente. Drain pasta in prepared colander, reserving cooking water.
- Add pepper and 2 cups reserved cooking water to Dutch oven with fat and bring to boil over high heat. Boil rapidly, scraping up any browned bits, until emulsified and reduced to 1 1/2 cups, about 5 minutes. (If you've reduced it too far, add more reserved cooking water to equal 1 1/2 cups.)
- Reduce heat to low, add pasta and pancetta, and stir to evenly coat. Add Pecorino and stir until cheese is melted and sauce is slightly thickened, about 1 minute. Off heat, adjust sauce consistency with remaining reserved cooking water as needed. Transfer to platter and serve immediately, passing extra pepper and Pecorino separately.

---

Guanciale is traditional; if you can find it, use it and increase the browning time to 10 to 12 minutes. Have pancetta cut to order at the deli counter; avoid pre-sliced or pre-diced products. Serve in slightly smaller portions alongside a green vegetable or salad.
