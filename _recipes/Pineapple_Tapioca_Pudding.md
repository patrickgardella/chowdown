ingredients:
- 1/4 cup Minute Tapioca
- 4 T sugar
- 1/4 t salt
- 1 egg yolk
- 4 cups milk
- 4 T butter
- 1/2 cup brown sugar, firmly packed
- 1 cup crushed pineapple
- 1/3 cup broken pecan pieces
- 1 egg white, stiffly beaten
- Whipped cream, for serving

directions:
- Combine Minute Tapioca, sugar, salt, egg yolk, and milk in the top of a double boiler. Stir enough to break the egg yolk.
- Place over rapidly boiling water, bring to scalding point (allow 5–7 minutes) and cook 5 minutes, stirring frequently. Remove from heat.
- Melt butter in a saucepan. Add brown sugar and cook until completely dissolved and bubbling.
- Add pineapple and pecans to the butter mixture, then stir the whole thing into the tapioca.
- Fold a small amount of the tapioca into the stiff egg white, then gradually fold in the remaining tapioca.
- Chill — mixture thickens as it cools. Garnish with whipped cream to serve.
