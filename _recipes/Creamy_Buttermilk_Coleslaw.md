ingredients:
- 1 lb red or green cabbage, shredded (6 cups)
- 1 1/4 tsp table salt, divided
- 1 large carrot, peeled and shredded
- 1/2 cup buttermilk
- 2 Tbsp mayonnaise
- 2 Tbsp sour cream
- 1 small shallot, minced
- 2 Tbsp minced fresh parsley
- 1/2 tsp cider vinegar
- 1/4 tsp Dijon mustard
- 1/2 tsp sugar
- 1/8 tsp pepper

directions:
- Toss cabbage with 1 teaspoon salt in colander set over medium bowl. Let sit until cabbage wilts, at least 1 hour or up to 4 hours. Rinse cabbage under cold running water. Press but do not squeeze to drain; pat dry with paper towels. Transfer to large bowl; add carrot.
- Combine buttermilk, mayonnaise, sour cream, shallot, parsley, vinegar, mustard, sugar, pepper, and remaining 1/4 teaspoon salt in small bowl. Pour dressing over cabbage and carrot and toss to coat. Serve chilled or at room temperature.

---

Can be refrigerated for up to 2 days. To serve immediately, rinse salted cabbage in a large bowl of ice water, drain, pick out ice cubes, then pat dry before dressing.
