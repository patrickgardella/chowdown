ingredients:
- 2 T Minute Tapioca
- 1/4 t salt
- 1/8 t pepper
- 3/4 cup milk
- 1 T butter
- 4 egg yolks, beaten until thick and lemon-colored
- 4 egg whites, stiffly beaten

directions:
- Combine Minute Tapioca, salt, pepper, and milk in the top of a double boiler.
- Place over rapidly boiling water, bring to scalding point (allow 3–5 minutes) and cook 5 minutes, stirring frequently.
- Add butter. Remove from heat and let cool slightly.
- Add egg yolks to the tapioca mixture and beat well. Fold in the stiff egg whites.
- Pour into a hot, buttered 10-inch frying pan. Cook over low heat for 3 minutes.
- Bake in a moderate oven (350°F) for 15 minutes, until a knife inserted in the center comes out clean.
- Fold carefully and serve on a hot platter.
