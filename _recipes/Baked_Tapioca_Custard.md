ingredients:
- 1/2 cup Minute Tapioca
- 1/2 cup sugar
- 1/4 t salt
- 4 cups milk
- 3 eggs, lightly beaten
- 1 T butter
- Nutmeg

directions:
- Combine Minute Tapioca, sugar, salt, and milk in the top of a double boiler.
- Place over rapidly boiling water, bring to scalding point (allow 5–7 minutes) and cook 5 minutes, stirring frequently.
- Temper the eggs by pouring the hot tapioca mixture slowly over the beaten eggs while stirring vigorously.
- Add butter.
- Pour into a greased baking dish and sprinkle with nutmeg.
- Place dish in a pan of hot water and bake in a slow oven (325°F) for 1 hour, or until a knife inserted in the center comes out clean.
