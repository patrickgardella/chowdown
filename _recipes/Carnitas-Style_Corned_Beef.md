ingredients:
- "**Crispy corned beef:**"
- 1 (3.5–4 lb) corned beef brisket, with spice packet
- 1 large yellow onion, sliced
- 4 cloves garlic, smashed
- 1 (12-oz) bottle stout or amber beer (or beef broth)
- 1 cup water
- 1 bay leaf
- 1 tsp black peppercorns
- 1 tsp smoked paprika
- Freshly-ground black pepper
- "**Optional gravy:**"
- 3 Tbsp butter
- 3 Tbsp all-purpose flour
- 2 1/2 cups strained cooking broth

directions:
- Cut corned beef into 3-inch chunks. Place sliced onion and smashed garlic in the bottom of the Instant Pot. Set the corned beef on top, sprinkled with the spice packet. Pour in the beer and water, then add the bay leaf, peppercorns, and smoked paprika.
- Lock the lid and cook on High Pressure for 100 minutes. Allow the pressure to naturally release for 15 minutes, then carefully quick-release any remaining pressure.
- Transfer the corned beef to a cutting board and let it rest for about 10 minutes. Then shred into large, chunky pieces (not too fine — bigger pieces crisp best).
- "**Optional gravy:** Strain the cooking liquid through a fine-mesh strainer into a bowl; discard solids. Press Sauté on the Instant Pot. Add butter and let it melt, then whisk in flour and cook for about 1 minute, stirring constantly, until lightly golden. Slowly whisk in about 2 1/2 cups strained broth until lump-free. Simmer 2–3 minutes until thickened. Season with pepper."
- Preheat the oven broiler to high. Spread shredded beef in an even layer on a baking sheet. Drizzle with 3–4 tablespoons of cooking liquid (or melted butter) and add a few cracks of black pepper. Broil for 4–5 minutes, then gently toss and broil for another 2–4 minutes, until edges are browned and crispy but the meat stays juicy. Serve warm.

---

Slow cooker: Cook on low 8–9 hours or high 4–5 hours. Stovetop: Simmer in a covered Dutch oven over low heat for 3–3 1/2 hours until fork-tender. Then broil as directed.
