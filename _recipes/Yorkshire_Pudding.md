ingredients:
- 2 Tbsp olive oil
- 2 egg whites
- 3 whole eggs
- 1 cup all-purpose flour
- 1/2 cup milk
- 2 Tbsp cold water

directions:
- Preheat oven to 450°F.
- Drop 1 teaspoon olive oil into each of 6 muffin cups of a muffin tin.
- Beat eggs, egg whites, and milk together in a glass bowl until smooth. Heat the egg mixture in a microwave for 1 minute.
- Stir flour into the egg mixture until completely incorporated into a batter.
- Preheat muffin cups in the oven until the oil is hot and smoky, 2–3 minutes. Heat is critical — Yorkshire pudding must cook in HOT muffin tins.
- Stir cold water into batter then pour 1/4 cup of batter into each muffin cup.
- Bake until risen and cooked in the center, about 25 minutes. Serve immediately.

A family recipe from Julia Brooks Mills, learned from a local Brit in post-WWII England. If the muffin tin and oven are not sufficiently hot, the puddings won't rise.
