ingredients:
- 1/4 cup table salt, for brining
- 1/4 cup sugar, for brining
- 1 (2- to 2 1/2-lb) boneless pork loin roast, trimmed
- 2 oz salt pork, chopped
- 3 cups whole milk
- 5 garlic cloves, peeled
- 1 Tbsp finely chopped fresh sage
- 1/4 tsp baking soda
- 1/2 cup dry white wine
- 3 Tbsp finely chopped fresh parsley, divided
- 1 tsp Dijon mustard

directions:
- Dissolve 1/4 cup salt and sugar in 2 quarts cold water in large container. Submerge roast in brine, cover, and refrigerate for at least 1 1/2 hours or up to 2 hours. Remove roast from brine and pat dry with paper towels.
- Adjust oven rack to middle position and heat oven to 275°F. Bring salt pork and 1/2 cup water to simmer in Dutch oven over medium heat. Simmer until water evaporates and salt pork begins to sizzle, 5 to 6 minutes. Continue to cook, stirring frequently, until salt pork is lightly browned and fat has rendered, 2 to 3 minutes. Using slotted spoon, discard salt pork, leaving fat in pot.
- Increase heat to medium-high, add roast to pot, and brown on all sides, 8 to 10 minutes. Transfer roast to large plate. Add milk, garlic, sage, and baking soda to pot and bring to simmer, scraping up any browned bits. Cook, stirring frequently, until milk is lightly browned, 14 to 16 minutes. Reduce heat to medium-low and continue to cook, stirring constantly, until milk thickens to consistency of thin batter, 1 to 3 minutes. Remove pot from heat.
- Return roast to pot, cover, and transfer to oven. Cook until center registers 140°F, 40 to 50 minutes, flipping roast halfway through. Transfer roast to carving board, tent with foil, and let rest for 20 to 25 minutes.
- Add wine to pot and return sauce to simmer over medium-high heat, whisking vigorously to smooth out sauce. Simmer until sauce has consistency of thin batter, 1 to 3 minutes. Off heat, stir in 2 tablespoons parsley and season with salt and pepper. Slice roast into 1/4-inch-thick slices, sprinkle with remaining 1 tablespoon parsley, and serve with sauce spooned over slices.

---

The milk will bubble up when added to the pot — remove from heat and stir to break up foam if needed. If using enhanced (injected) pork, skip the brine and start at step 2.
