ingredients:
- "**Spice rub:**"
- 2 Tbsp paprika
- 2 Tbsp packed light brown sugar
- 1 Tbsp table salt
- 2 tsp chili powder
- 1 1/2 tsp pepper
- 1 1/2 tsp garlic powder
- 1 1/2 tsp onion powder
- 1 1/2 tsp cayenne pepper (reduce to 1/2 tsp for less heat)
- 1/2 tsp dried thyme
- "**Ribs:**"
- 2 (2 1/2- to 3-lb) racks St. Louis-style spareribs, trimmed
- 1/2 cup apple juice
- 3 Tbsp cider vinegar
- 3/4 cup wood chips, soaked in water 15 minutes and drained
- 1 disposable aluminum roasting pan (charcoal) or 2 disposable pie plates (gas)

directions:
- Combine all spice rub ingredients in bowl. Rub 2 tablespoons spice rub on each side of each rack of ribs. Let ribs sit at room temperature while preparing grill. Combine apple juice and vinegar in small bowl and set aside.
- "**For charcoal:** Open bottom vent halfway and evenly space 15 unlit charcoal briquettes on 1 side of grill. Place disposable pan filled with 2 cups water on other side. Light large chimney starter one-third filled with charcoal (2 quarts). When top coals are partially covered with ash, pour evenly over unlit coals. Sprinkle soaked wood chips over lit coals. Set cooking grate in place, cover, and open lid vent halfway. Heat grill until hot and chips are smoking, about 5 minutes."
- "**For gas:** Place soaked wood chips in pie plate filled with 1/4 cup water and set over primary burner. Place second pie plate filled with 2 cups water on other burner(s). Turn all burners to high, cover, and heat until hot and chips are smoking, about 15 minutes. Turn primary burner to medium-high and turn off other burner(s). Adjust to maintain 250 to 275°F."
- Clean and oil cooking grate. Place ribs meat side down on cooler side of grill over water-filled pan. Cover (positioning lid vent over meat if using charcoal) and cook until ribs are deep red and smoky, about 1 1/2 hours, brushing with apple juice mixture occasionally throughout cooking.
- Transfer ribs to serving platter, tent with aluminum foil, and let rest for 10 minutes. Serve.
