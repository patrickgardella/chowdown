ingredients:
- "**Chicken:**"
- 2 Tbsp packed dark brown sugar
- 1 1/2 Tbsp kosher salt
- 1 1/2 tsp onion powder
- 1 1/2 tsp garlic powder
- 1 1/4 tsp paprika
- 1/4 tsp cayenne pepper
- 6 lbs bone-in chicken pieces (split breasts and/or leg quarters), trimmed
- "**Sauce:**"
- 1 cup ketchup
- 5 Tbsp molasses
- 3 Tbsp cider vinegar
- 2 Tbsp Worcestershire sauce
- 2 Tbsp Dijon mustard
- 1/2 tsp pepper
- 2 Tbsp vegetable oil
- 1 small onion, chopped fine
- 2 garlic cloves, minced
- 1 tsp chili powder
- 1/4 tsp cayenne pepper
- "**Grill:**"
- 1 large disposable aluminum roasting pan (charcoal) or 2 disposable aluminum pie plates (gas)

directions:
- Combine sugar, salt, onion powder, garlic powder, paprika, and cayenne in bowl. Arrange chicken skin side up on rimmed baking sheet and sprinkle both sides evenly with spice rub.
- Whisk ketchup, molasses, vinegar, Worcestershire, mustard, and pepper together in bowl. Heat oil in medium saucepan over medium heat until shimmering. Add onion and garlic; cook until onion is softened, 2 to 4 minutes. Add chili powder and cayenne and cook until fragrant, about 30 seconds. Whisk in ketchup mixture and bring to boil. Reduce heat to medium-low and simmer gently for 5 minutes. Set aside 1/3 cup sauce to baste chicken and reserve remaining sauce for serving.
- "**For charcoal:** Open bottom vent halfway and place disposable pan filled with 3 cups water on 1 side of grill. Light large chimney starter filled with charcoal briquettes (6 quarts). When top coals are partially covered with ash, pour evenly over other half of grill (opposite disposable pan). Set cooking grate in place, cover, and open lid vent halfway. Heat grill until hot, about 5 minutes."
- "**For gas:** Place 2 disposable pie plates, each filled with 1 1/2 cups water, directly on 1 burner (opposite primary burner). Turn all burners to high, cover, and heat until hot, about 15 minutes. Turn primary burner to medium-high and turn off other burner(s). Adjust primary burner as needed to maintain 325 to 350°F."
- Clean and oil cooking grate. Place chicken skin side down over hotter part of grill and cook until browned and blistered in spots, 2 to 5 minutes. Flip and cook until second side is browned, 4 to 6 minutes. Move chicken to cooler side of grill and brush both sides with the basting sauce. Arrange chicken skin side up with leg quarters closest to fire and breasts farthest away. Cover and cook for 25 minutes.
- Brush both sides of chicken with remaining basting sauce and continue to cook, covered, until breasts register 160°F and leg quarters register 175°F, 25 to 35 minutes more.
- Transfer to serving platter, tent with foil, and let rest for 10 minutes. Serve, passing reserved sauce separately.

---

Move chicken away from any flare-ups when browning over the hotter side. Try to select similar-size chicken parts for even cooking.
