ingredients:
- "**Tapioca cream:**"
- 3 T Minute Tapioca
- 3 T sugar
- 1/4 t salt
- 1 egg yolk
- 2 cups milk
- 1/4 t grated orange zest
- 1/4 t grated lemon zest
- 1 egg white, stiffly beaten
- "**Citrus sauce:**"
- 1 orange
- 1 lemon
- Water (enough to make 1/4 cup combined with the fruit juice)
- 2/3 cup sugar

directions:
- Combine Minute Tapioca, sugar, salt, egg yolk, and milk in the top of a double boiler. Stir enough to break the egg yolk.
- Place over rapidly boiling water, bring to scalding point (allow 5–7 minutes) and cook 5 minutes, stirring frequently.
- Remove from heat and stir in grated orange and lemon zest.
- Fold a small amount of the tapioca into the stiff egg white, then gradually blend in the remaining tapioca. Chill — mixture thickens as it cools.
- "**For the citrus sauce:** Remove sections from the orange and lemon, discarding all membrane. Drain, reserving juice."
- Add water to juice to make 1/4 cup liquid. Stir in 2/3 cup sugar until dissolved.
- Boil rapidly 4 minutes. Add fruit sections and boil gently 5 minutes more. Chill thoroughly.
- Serve in sherbet glasses, spooning a small amount of citrus sauce over each portion just before serving.
