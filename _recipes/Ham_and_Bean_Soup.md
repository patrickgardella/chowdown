ingredients:
- 2 Tbsp bacon grease (or 1 Tbsp extra virgin olive oil)
- 3/4 cup chopped carrots
- 3/4 cup chopped celery
- 3/4 cup chopped onion
- 1 Tbsp minced garlic
- Salt and pepper
- 1/8 tsp dried thyme
- 1 Tbsp flour
- 2 cans chicken broth (or equivalent stock)
- 1 chicken bouillon cube
- 1/4 tsp red pepper flakes
- 1/2 tsp dried mustard
- 1/2 bay leaf
- 8 oz diced or cubed ham
- 2 cans great northern or navy beans, drained and rinsed

directions:
- Heat grease (or oil) in a soup pot over medium heat.
- Add carrots, celery, and onions with a pinch of salt. Sweat until soft, about 10 minutes.
- Add garlic and thyme; stir until fragrant.
- Add flour and stir to coat the vegetables.
- Add broth, bouillon cube, red pepper flakes, mustard, and bay leaf. Bring to a boil.
- Add ham and beans. Reduce to a simmer and cook 20–30 minutes. Remove bay leaf before serving.
