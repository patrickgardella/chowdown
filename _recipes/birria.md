ingredients:
- "**For the birria:**"
- 4½ oz guajillo chilies (about 16), stemmed
- 7 morita chilies, stemmed
- 2 ancho chilies, stemmed
- 1 plum tomato
- 4 garlic cloves, peeled
- 2 tsp Dominican oregano
- 2 tsp ground cumin
- 1 tsp black peppercorns
- 1 tsp whole cloves
- 1 bay leaf
- ¼ white onion, peeled
- ¼ cup kosher salt
- 5 Tbsp white vinegar
- 5 lbs beef round knuckle, cap removed
- Kosher salt and freshly ground black pepper, to taste
- 1⅔ cups vegetable oil
- "**For the tacos and serving:**"
- 1 lb shredded Monterey Jack cheese
- 1 (27.5-oz) package corn tortillas
- 1 bunch cilantro, minced
- 1 large white onion, finely chopped
- Lime wedges, for serving
- Salsas of choice, for serving

directions:
- In a medium saucepan, cover the chilies with 8 cups water. Bring to a boil, remove from heat, and let sit until chilies are soft, about 10 minutes. Strain, reserving the liquid.
- Boil the tomato in a small saucepan for 2 minutes, then strain. Transfer tomato and chilies to a blender with 4 cups of reserved soaking liquid, garlic, oregano, cumin, peppercorns, cloves, bay leaf, onion, salt, and vinegar. Blend until smooth.
- Rinse the beef, pat dry, and cut into 2-inch pieces. Season all over with salt and pepper.
- Heat oil in a large saucepan over medium-high. Add the chili paste and cook, stirring constantly, until bright red, 15–20 minutes. Add the beef and 4 cups water. Bring to a boil, then reduce to a simmer. Cook until beef is very tender, about 3 hours.
- Skim the bright red oil from the top of the birria and set aside.
- Remove all meat from the consommé and finely chop (you should have about 7 cups). Return about 1½ cups of meat to the consommé and keep warm. Add a splash of broth to the remaining meat to keep it moist.
- Heat a large nonstick skillet over medium. Dip a tortilla in the reserved red oil to coat. Place in the skillet and cook until pliable, 1–2 minutes. Top half with cheese and about ¼ cup chopped meat. Fold over and cook, flipping once, until golden and crispy, 3–4 minutes. Repeat with remaining tortillas.
- To serve, ladle consommé into a small cup. Sprinkle tacos with cilantro and onion, squeeze in lime juice, and dip in the consommé.
