ingredients:
- "**Fruit:**"
- 8 cups (40 oz) strawberries, hulled, divided
- 6 Tbsp sugar
- "**Shortcake:**"
- 2 cups all-purpose flour
- 5 Tbsp sugar, divided
- 1 Tbsp baking powder
- 1/2 tsp table salt
- 8 Tbsp unsalted butter, cut into 1/2-inch pieces and chilled
- 1/2 cup plus 1 Tbsp half-and-half or milk, divided
- 1 large egg, lightly beaten, plus 1 large egg white, lightly beaten
- "**Whipped cream:**"
- 1 cup heavy cream, chilled
- 1 Tbsp sugar
- 1 tsp vanilla extract

directions:
- Crush 3 cups strawberries in large bowl with potato masher. Slice remaining 5 cups of berries and stir into crushed berries along with sugar. Set aside until sugar has dissolved and berries are juicy, at least 30 minutes or up to 2 hours.
- Adjust oven rack to lower-middle position and heat oven to 425°F. Line baking sheet with parchment paper. Pulse flour, 3 tablespoons sugar, baking powder, and salt in food processor until combined. Sprinkle butter pieces over top and pulse until mixture resembles coarse meal, about 15 pulses. Transfer mixture to large bowl.
- Whisk half-and-half and lightly beaten egg together in small bowl, then stir into flour mixture with rubber spatula until large clumps form. Turn dough onto lightly floured work surface and knead lightly until it comes together.
- Pat dough into 9 by 6-inch rectangle, about 3/4 inch thick. Do not overwork dough. Using floured 2 1/2-inch biscuit cutter, cut out 6 dough rounds. Arrange shortcakes on prepared sheet, spaced about 1 1/2 inches apart. Brush tops with lightly beaten egg white and sprinkle evenly with remaining 2 tablespoons sugar.
- Bake until shortcakes are golden brown, 12 to 14 minutes, rotating sheet halfway through. Transfer to wire rack and let cool until warm, about 10 minutes.
- Whip heavy cream, sugar, and vanilla together in a chilled bowl until soft peaks form.
- Split shortcakes in half and spoon strawberries over the bottom halves. Top with a dollop of whipped cream and cover with the top halves. Serve immediately.

---

Start the recipe by preparing the fruit and set it aside while preparing the biscuits to allow the juices to become syrupy. Unbaked shortcakes can be covered with plastic wrap and refrigerated for up to 2 hours.
