ingredients:

- 4 split chicken breasts
- 8 slices of cheese (muenster or provolone)
- 1 can creamy chicken mushroom soup
- 1/4 c milk
- 1 1/2 c stuffing mix
- 1/4 c butter, melted

directions:
- preheat oven to 350
- arrange chicken in 13 x 9 in dish
- top with cheese slices
- combine soup and milk and spoon over chicken
- sprinkle stuffing mix all over
- drizzle butter on top
- bake for 1 hour or until chicken reaches 165
