ingredients:
- "**Poolish (make 18–24 hours ahead):**"
- 50g (1/4 cup) water
- Pinch instant yeast
- 50g (1/4 cup + 1 Tbsp) all-purpose flour
- "**Dough:**"
- 360g (1.5 cups) warm water, 86°F / 30°C
- 6g (1 tsp) instant yeast
- 20g (1.5 Tbsp) olive oil
- 20g (1⅔ Tbsp) sugar
- 100g ripe yeasted poolish (from above)
- 630g (5 cups) all-purpose flour, 11.7% protein
- 16g (2¾ tsp) salt

directions:
- "**Make the poolish 18–24 hours ahead:** Combine water, yeast, and flour. Stir to combine. Cover and let sit at room temperature for 18–24 hours until bubbly."
- Combine all dough ingredients in a stand mixer with a dough hook. Mix on medium-low until a ball forms.
- Transfer to a floured surface and knead by hand 2–3 minutes until smooth and passing the tug test. Round into a ball, cover, and let rise at room temperature 60 minutes.
- Divide into 4 equal pieces and round each into a ball. Place on an oiled tray, cover tightly, and refrigerate 24–48 hours.
- Allow dough to come to room temperature for 1 hour before shaping. Preheat oven with pizza steel or stone to 550°F / 290°C. Stretch and shape each ball into a 12-inch round.
