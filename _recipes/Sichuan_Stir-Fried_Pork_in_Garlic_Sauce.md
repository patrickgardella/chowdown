ingredients:
- "**Sauce:**"
- 1/2 cup chicken broth
- 2 Tbsp sugar
- 2 Tbsp soy sauce
- 4 tsp Chinese black vinegar
- 1 Tbsp toasted sesame oil
- 1 Tbsp Chinese rice wine or dry sherry
- 2 tsp ketchup
- 2 tsp fish sauce
- 2 tsp cornstarch
- "**Pork:**"
- 12 oz boneless country-style pork ribs, trimmed
- 1 tsp baking soda
- 2 Tbsp Chinese rice wine or dry sherry
- 2 Tbsp cornstarch
- "**Stir-fry:**"
- 2 scallions, white parts minced, green parts sliced thin, divided
- 2 Tbsp Asian broad-bean chili paste
- 4 garlic cloves, minced
- 1/4 cup vegetable oil, divided
- 6 oz shiitake mushrooms, stemmed and sliced thin
- 2 celery ribs, cut on bias into 1/4-inch slices

directions:
- Whisk all sauce ingredients together in bowl; set aside.
- Cut pork into 2-inch lengths, then cut each length into 1/4-inch matchsticks. Combine pork with 1/2 cup cold water and baking soda in bowl. Let sit at room temperature for 15 minutes. Rinse pork in cold water. Drain well and pat dry with paper towels. Whisk rice wine and cornstarch together in bowl. Add pork and toss to coat.
- Combine scallion whites, chili paste, and garlic in bowl.
- Heat 1 tablespoon vegetable oil in 12-inch nonstick skillet over high heat until just smoking. Add mushrooms and cook, stirring frequently, until tender, 2 to 4 minutes. Add celery and continue to cook until celery is crisp-tender, 2 to 4 minutes. Transfer vegetables to separate bowl.
- Add remaining 3 tablespoons vegetable oil to now-empty skillet and place over medium-low heat. Add scallion-garlic mixture and cook, stirring frequently, until fragrant, about 30 seconds. Transfer 1 tablespoon scallion-garlic oil to small bowl and set aside. Add pork to skillet and cook, stirring frequently, until no longer pink, 3 to 5 minutes.
- Whisk sauce mixture to recombine and add to skillet. Increase heat to high and cook, stirring constantly, until sauce is thickened and pork is cooked through, 1 to 2 minutes. Return vegetables to skillet and toss to combine. Transfer to serving platter, sprinkle with scallion greens and reserved scallion-garlic oil, and serve.

---

Serve with white rice. If Chinese black vinegar is unavailable, substitute 2 tsp balsamic vinegar and 2 tsp rice vinegar. If Asian broad-bean chili paste is unavailable, substitute 2 tsp Asian chili-garlic paste or sriracha.
