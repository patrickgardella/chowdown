ingredients:
- "**Topping:**"
- 2 lbs Granny Smith or Golden Delicious apples, peeled and cored
- 4 Tbsp unsalted butter, cut into 4 pieces
- 1/2 cup packed light brown sugar
- 2 tsp lemon juice
- "**Cake:**"
- 1 cup all-purpose flour
- 1 Tbsp cornmeal (optional)
- 1 tsp baking powder
- 1/2 tsp salt
- 3/4 cup granulated sugar
- 1/4 cup packed light brown sugar
- 2 large eggs
- 6 Tbsp unsalted butter, melted and slightly cooled
- 1/2 cup sour cream
- 1 tsp vanilla extract

directions:
- Adjust oven rack to lowest position and heat oven to 350°F. Grease a 9-inch round cake pan.
- Cut half of apples into 1/4-inch-thick slices; cut remaining apples into 1/2-inch-thick slices. Melt butter in a 12-inch skillet over medium-high heat. Add the 1/2-inch-thick slices and cook, stirring occasionally, until they begin to caramelize, 4 to 6 minutes (do not fully cook). Add brown sugar and lemon juice and cook, stirring constantly, until sugar dissolves and apples are juicy and coated, about 1 minute. Transfer apple mixture to prepared pan and lightly press into an even layer.
- Whisk flour, cornmeal (if using), baking powder, and salt together in a bowl. Whisk granulated sugar, brown sugar, and eggs in a large bowl until thick, about 45 seconds. Slowly whisk in melted butter until combined. Whisk in sour cream and vanilla until combined. Whisk in flour mixture until just combined. Transfer batter to prepared pan and spread evenly over apples.
- Bake until golden brown and a toothpick inserted in center comes out clean, 35 to 40 minutes, rotating pan halfway through.
- Let cake cool in pan on wire rack for 20 minutes. Run a thin knife around the edge of the pan to loosen. Invert cake onto a rack set in a rimmed baking sheet. Let sit until cake releases from the pan, about 1 minute. Remove pan, scrape off any fruit stuck in the pan and arrange on top, and let cool at least 20 more minutes before serving.

---

Variations: Add 1/3 cup finely ground toasted almonds to flour and 1 tsp almond extract with the sour cream and vanilla. Or add 1 tsp finely grated lemon zest and 1 tsp finely chopped fresh thyme with the sour cream and vanilla.
