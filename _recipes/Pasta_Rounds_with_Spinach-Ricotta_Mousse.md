ingredients:
- "**Mousse:**"
- 1 lb ricotta cheese
- 1/2 lb mascarpone cheese
- 1 bunch spinach (about 1/2 lb), washed, dried, and stemmed
- 1 Tbsp unsalted butter
- 1/4 cup pine nuts
- 3 cups (12 oz) grated mozzarella cheese
- 1 oz dry-aged goat cheese, grated
- 1 egg, lightly beaten
- 1 tsp kosher salt
- 1 tsp freshly ground white pepper
- "**Pasta:**"
- 3/4 recipe (18 oz) Basic Pasta Dough (or Herb Pasta Dough)
- Semolina or all-purpose flour, for dusting
- 2 Tbsp unsalted butter
- 1/3 cup freshly grated Parmesan cheese
- 3 cups Tomato Garlic Basil Sauce

directions:
- "**Make the mousse:** In a food processor fitted with the steel blade, purée the ricotta, mascarpone, and half of the spinach. Transfer to a medium mixing bowl."
- "**Prepare the spinach:** Melt 1 tablespoon butter in a small skillet. Sauté remaining spinach just until wilted, 2 to 3 minutes. Coarsely chop and let cool."
- Toast pine nuts in a small skillet over medium heat, stirring constantly, about 2 minutes. Cool and fold into the cheese mixture with the cooled spinach, mozzarella, goat cheese, egg, salt, and pepper. Adjust seasoning and set aside.
- "**Make the pasta rounds:** Cut pasta dough into 4 pieces. On a surface dusted lightly with flour, roll out the first piece to a rectangle 20 inches long and 4 inches wide. Trim the rectangle to even the sides. Spread 1/4 of the mousse over the lower part of the length of the dough. Roll the dough over the filling, enclosing it completely, forming a 20 by 1-inch pasta roll. Cut the roll into approximately 1-inch pieces, yielding 20 slices. Repeat with remaining dough and mousse."
- Preheat oven to 375°F. Melt 2 tablespoons butter and brush eight 4-inch tart pans. Arrange 10 of the pasta slices in a circle, cut side up, in each pan (it will be a tight squeeze) and sprinkle each circle with grated Parmesan. Set pans on a baking tray and bake for 15 minutes. Invert each pasta round out of the tart pans onto the tray and continue to bake until both sides are golden brown and crisp, about 15 to 20 minutes more.
- Heat the sauce and spoon a little in the center of each warmed plate. Set one of the rounds on the sauce and serve immediately, passing remaining sauce in a small bowl.

---

Make ahead through the unbaked step — arrange pasta slices in pans, refrigerate covered, and remove 30 minutes before baking.
