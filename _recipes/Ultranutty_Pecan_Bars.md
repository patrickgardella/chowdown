ingredients:
- "**Crust:**"
- 1 3/4 cups (8 3/4 oz) all-purpose flour
- 6 Tbsp (2 5/8 oz) granulated sugar
- 1/2 tsp table salt
- 8 Tbsp unsalted butter, melted
- "**Topping:**"
- 1/3 cup packed (5 1/4 oz) light brown sugar
- 1/2 cup light corn syrup
- 7 Tbsp unsalted butter, melted and hot
- 1 tsp vanilla extract
- 1 tsp table salt
- 4 cups (1 lb) pecan halves, toasted
- 1/2 tsp flake sea salt (optional)

directions:
- Adjust oven rack to lowest position and heat oven to 350°F. Make foil sling for 13 by 9-inch baking pan by folding 2 long sheets of foil — first sheet should be 13 inches wide, and second sheet should be 9 inches wide. Lay sheets perpendicular to each other in pan with extra foil hanging over edges. Push foil into corners and up sides, smoothing flush to pan. Grease foil.
- Whisk flour, sugar, and salt together in bowl. Stir in melted butter with wooden spoon until dough begins to form. Using your hands, combine until no dry flour remains and small portion of dough holds together when squeezed. Scatter 1-tablespoon pieces of dough evenly over surface of pan. Using your fingertips and palm, press and smooth dough into even thickness in bottom of pan.
- Bake until edges are beginning to brown, 16 to 18 minutes. Remove pan from oven and increase oven temperature to 375°F.
- Whisk brown sugar, corn syrup, melted butter, vanilla, and table salt in bowl until smooth (mixture will look separated at first but will become homogeneous), about 20 seconds. Fold pecans into sugar mixture until nuts are evenly coated.
- Pour topping over crust and spread into even layer with spatula, pushing to edges and into corners (there will be bare patches). Bake until topping is evenly distributed and rapidly bubbling across entire surface, 23 to 25 minutes.
- Transfer pan to wire rack and sprinkle bars with sea salt, if using. Let bars cool completely in pan, about 1 1/2 hours. Using foil overhang, remove bars from pan. Cut into 24 pieces before serving.

---

Toast pecans on a rimmed baking sheet in a 350°F oven until fragrant, 8 to 12 minutes, shaking the sheet halfway through. Use pecan halves, not pieces. Bars can be stored at room temperature for up to 5 days.
